Privacy policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

In short: we only collect the details you type into our upside calculator, we use them only to send you your estimate and follow up about it, we keep them for at most 2 years, and we don't use cookies or tracking on this website.

1. Who we are

This website, peazypricing.com, is run by [Company name], [registered address], Belgium, enterprise number [KBO/BCE number] ("Peazy", "we", "us"). We are the controller of the personal data described in this policy.

For anything related to your personal data, email timo@peazypricing.com.

2. What we collect and why

WhatWhyLegal basis
Upside calculator: name, company, job title, work email, phone (optional), industry, annual revenue, and the estimate we calculate To send you your estimate and contact you about it, for example to offer a walkthrough of how Peazy could work for you Your consent (GDPR art. 6(1)(a)), which you give by ticking the box in the calculator

3. Cookies

This website does not set any cookies and does not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools. That's why you don't see a cookie banner. If that ever changes, we will update this policy and ask for your consent before setting any non-essential cookies.

4. Who we share it with

We share personal data only with the service providers we need to run this website and handle your request:

  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) forwards what you enter in the upside calculator to our mailbox.
  • Our email provider stores the emails we receive and send.
  • GitHub (GitHub, Inc., USA) hosts this website through GitHub Pages and may log visitors' IP addresses for security purposes.
  • Google Fonts (Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd.) serves the fonts on this website. Your browser connects to Google's servers to load them, which shares your IP address with Google.

Some of these providers may process data outside the European Economic Area, for example in the United States. Where that happens, the transfer relies on an adequacy decision such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, or on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

5. How long we keep it

We keep calculator submissions and related emails for up to 2 years after our last contact with you, then delete them. If you become a customer, we keep what we need for the contract and for our legal and accounting obligations for as long as those require. If you withdraw your consent or ask us to delete your data, we do so sooner (see section 6).

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and get a copy of it
  • have inaccurate data corrected
  • have your data deleted
  • restrict how we use your data, or object to our use of it
  • receive the data you gave us in a portable format
  • withdraw your consent at any time. This does not affect anything we did with your data before you withdrew it

To use any of these rights, email timo@peazypricing.com. We will reply within one month.

You can also lodge a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority (Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit / Autorité de protection des données), Drukpersstraat 35 / Rue de la Presse 35, 1000 Brussels, www.dataprotectionauthority.be. We'd appreciate the chance to sort it out with you first.

7. Security

This website is served over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, and we limit access to your data to the people at Peazy who need it to follow up with you.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our website or the way we handle data changes. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
