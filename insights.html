Questions about Insights

What data do you need?

Your invoice data. A Quick Scan starts from the same data and gives you a first picture within a week.

How fast can Insights be live?

Within weeks.

Do we need the other modules?

No. Insights works on its own and is where most companies start. You can add Price management and Quote later, on the same core.

Does Insights change our prices?

No. Insights shows you where to act. Your people decide what to do about it.
