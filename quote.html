Questions about Quote

Do we need Price management first?

Yes. Quote builds on the prices and guardrails you manage in Price management.

Do sales reps see our margins?

No. Sales sees prices and whether a deal is in range, not margins.

Does every quote need approval?

No. Deals inside the guardrails need no approval. Only exceptions are routed for sign-off.
