good to know

Frequently asked questions

Who is Peazy for?

B2B manufacturers and distributors who want to protect and grow their margin. Business owners and commercial leaders use it to see and steer margin, pricing teams to manage prices, and sales teams to quote.

Do we need to replace our ERP?

No. Peazy connects to the ERP you already have and works alongside it.

Do we need all modules?

No. Start with the one module that solves your most pressing problem and add the others when you're ready. They all run on the same core.

How fast can we be live?

Insights can be live within weeks, starting from your invoice data. A Quick Scan gives you your own value case in one week.

Does the AI change our prices?

Never on its own. AI helps map your data, guides the setup and coaches your sales reps. Your people make the decisions.

What does a Quick Scan involve?

You share your invoice data, and within a week you get your own version of the value case: where margin slips and what it is worth. It comes at a fixed fee.
